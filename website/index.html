SUBJECT: MISSION ARTEMIS VI -- DEPLOYMENT OF HUMANOID INSPECTOR UNIT "ARGUS-01"
TARGET OBJECTIVE: LOCATE AND INSPECT "MARK ROVER III" (DEPLOYED DURING ARTEMIS IV)
DATE OF RECORD: NOVEMBER 14, 1990
ORIGINATING SITE: MARE ACIDALIUM DEPRESSION, MARS (QUADRANGLE MC-04)
PRIMARY PAYLOAD STATUS: SIGNAL LOST - ANOMALOUS VISUAL CONTACT AT 14:02:11 UTC
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ RECOVERY SUMMARY ]
- ARTEMIS IV (1988): HEAVY CARGO DROP OF "MARK ROVER III" COMPLETED IN MARE ACIDALIUM.
  ROVER DEPLOYED TO QUADRANGLE MC-04 FOR GEOLOGICAL AND BIOSIGNATURE SURVEY.
- ARTEMIS V (1988): THREE-PERSON CREW TRANSPORTER LAUNCHED AUGUST 12. LANDED OCTOBER 29,
  APPROXIMATELY 4 KM FROM MARK ROVER III. RETURN-STAGE FAILURE ON NOVEMBER 9 LEFT CREW STRANDED.
  PUBLICLY DECLARED LOST ON NOVEMBER 16, 1988.
- ARTEMIS VI (1990): UNMANNED RECONNAISSANCE MISSION DEPLOYING ARGUS-01 TO THE ORIGINAL
  ARTEMIS V LANDING SITE. OBJECTIVES INCLUDED CREW STATUS VERIFICATION AND RECOVERY OF M-III
  SCIENTIFIC ASSETS AND SURFACE RECORDINGS.
- ARGUS-01 EYE-CAMERA FEED WAS RECORDED ON 3/4-INCH U-MATIC ANALOG VIDEO TAPE BEFORE
  AUTOMATED TRANSCEIVER DISRUPTION. PARTIAL RECORDING RECOVERED.
